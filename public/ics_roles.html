Woodstock NET ICS Roles

Fall 2026 Citywide Deployment Exercise

Incident Team Lead (ITL)

  • Reports to ECC, oversees all response operations for neighborhood team
  • Decides WHAT to do

Responsibilities

  • Incident Prioritization
  • Delegate all actions and tasks to Operations Chief
  • Team Safety
  • Action plan for each incident

Duties

  • Assigns personnel to key ICS positions as needed
  • Ensures that scribe signs in all team members at the beginning of the exercise, and signs them in and out during deployments and returns.
  • Reviews the list of Current Conditions and prioritizes which to act upon first.
  • Keeps plans flexible and incorporates any new information received during the course of the exercise.
  • Incorporates SUVs as possible to enhance your operations
  • Fields and responds to requests from PBEM via team AROs
  • Decides if SAR team findings warrant messages from the team to PBEM

Operations Chief

  • Reports to ITL
  • Decides HOW to do it

Responsibilities

  • Main point of contact for ITL
  • Keeps ITL focused on prioritization and removes all task work

Duties

  • Takes direction from ITL and puts into action
  • Communicates between ITL and team
  • Provides situational awareness to ITL by summarizing status of functions and teams
  • Assigns NET members to Search and Rescue teams and deploys them to their objectives.
  • Prioritizes needs and requests; assigns tasks
  • Ensures that all areas of operation have complete documentation

Scribes (for ITL and for other Incident Command roles)

  • Document activities, keep paperwork organized, manage ICS Forms (review NET forms 1, 2a, 2b, 3, and 4)
  • Help ITL and other roles in Incident Command keep track of teams and resources throughout the exercise; manage check-in / check-out of SAR teams
  • Help prepare situation reports to transfer duties between incoming and outgoing ITLs at shift change
  • Catalog and store reports from SAR teams, ARO, and ITL

Public Information Officer (PIO)

In a real deployment, the PIO serves as the primary point of contact between the Incident Management Team and the public, media, and partner agencies, ensuring timely, accurate, and coordinated information throughout incident operations. During an exercise, this mostly means answering questions from curious neighbors about the exercise or the NET program. The PIO during an exercise should NOT talk to the media; any media inquiries about the NET program should be referred to PBEM.

Neighborhood Liaisons

Because we have such a large group, with many participating neighborhoods, we will have Neighborhood Liaison roles within the Incident Command Center. Each Neighborhood Liaison will help dispatch, direct, and document the work of a Search and Rescue team from their own neighborhood (eg, a Creston-Kenilworth NL will handle communications between the CK Search and Rescue team and Incident Command).

FRS/GMRS Radio Lead

  • Coordinates intra-team communications using Family Radio Service (FRS) or General Mobile Radio Service (GMRS) radios (walkie-talkies)
  • Facilitates communication between the Incident Team Leader (ITL) and SAR teams in the field
  • Depending on the team's structure and ITL direction, communicates directly with the team's ARO

Logistics Lead

Responsibilities

  • Organize and manage supplies, equipment
  • Provide food, water, and medical support to team members
  • Oversee team members and volunteers assigned to assist with logistics & procurement
  • Document activity; keeps records of supplies and equipment regarding need, location and availability

Duties

  • Establishes a record keeping system for all equipment, supplies; track status of supplies and equipment
  • Anticipate needs of the team as response unfolds
  • Orient volunteers regarding the parameters of the assignment
  • Establish a self care area for team members to get food, water, medical attention and rest
  • Assist areas of operations in getting needed supplies and equipment

Medical Lead

  • Establishes treatment area and triages incoming patients
  • Documents activities on NET forms 5a (Patient Treatment Area Record) and 5b (Individual Treatment Record)

Volunteer Coordinator

  • Helps intake and manage SUVs (Spontaneous Unaffiliated Volunteers), makes sure they are signed in, and coordinates with the ITL to get them assigned to roles.
  • May answer questions from neighbors about the NET program
