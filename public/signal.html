How to join the SE NETs Signal group

Already have Signal on your phone? Message a group admin here. Tell us your name and what neighborhood you're from and we'll add you to the group.

Don't have Signal yet?

Signal is an encrypted text messaging platform. You can get messages on your phone (Android or iOS) and your computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux). It’s a group text chat but it works better cross-platform for mixed iOS/Android groups. To use it on a computer, you have to first install it on a phone.

Download: https://signal.org/download/
