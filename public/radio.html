Woodstock NET radio check-in guide

Before every monthly team meeting, we will do an organized FRS/GMRS radio check-in. Woodstock uses FRS Channel 7 to check in.

FRS/GMRS radio check-in generally happens in the 30-minute window before meeting start time. For our regular monthly meetings, the check-in happens between 6pm – 6:30pm, and Net Control is stationed at Woodstock Park. Times and locations may change, so review the event details to confirm check-in time and Net Control location.

Check in from wherever you are during that time. You can check in from home before you leave, and you can check in even if you’re not planning to come to the meeting. Or you can check in from your car or on foot on your way to the meeting. However, the check-in will only work if you are within radio range of Net Control. If you live down the hill to the West or South of the park, you may need to get closer to get within radio range. You can test along the way to see how close you need to get to hear traffic on the team channel.

How to check in

  • Make sure your radio’s battery is charged. Replace batteries if needed.
  • Turn on your radio, and tune it to channel 7.
  • Listen first, to confirm nobody else is talking. Wait for a break in traffic before you check in.
  • Hold the radio with the antenna pointed up. If other voices sound garbled or scratchy, or if you can’t hear anything, try moving to a different location (away from major obstacles, or get up higher if possible, or walk or drive closer to the park).
  • Press the PTT (Push-to-talk) button and hold it down.
  • Hold the button for 1 second before starting to talk, to ensure the beginning of your message is not cut off. Keep the button held down during your transmission.
  • Talk across the speaker rather than directly into it.
  • Use the format ‘Hey you, it’s me’ to check in.
    • “Incident Command, this is Rover team 1”
    • “Net Control, this is {my name}”
  • Release the PTT button when you are done talking.
  • Wait to be acknowledged by Net Control before saying anything else. Net Control will respond “Rover team 1, go ahead”, or they may say “Rover team 1, please stand by”.
  • In some cases, you may be able to hear other team members and the Net Control operator on the channel, but they can’t hear you. Or Net Control may be in another conversation that you can’t hear and may be unable to take your check-in at that moment. This is why you should always wait to confirm that your check-in was heard before proceeding with your message.
  • When you get a go ahead, continue your check-in by naming your location (cross streets). If one of the street names might easy to mistake for something else, spell the name using the phonetic alphabet.

Don’t have a radio yet? Tips on choosing a radio

  • Very brief explanation of the difference between FRS and GMRS radios.
  • FRS does not require a license. A GMRS license costs $35 and lasts 10 years. No test required. One license covers a whole family.
  • Many of us on the Woodstock team have the Midland GXT1000. These are also the radios used as demos in the Basic NET training. This model also has GMRS capability which requires a license.
  • However, any FRS or GMRS radio works! No need to buy something new if you already have one at home. Quality devices sell for around $70 a pair; look for sales. BiMart has several different models that range from about $35 for a pair up to $80/pair.
  • A Ham radio is different from an FRS/GMRS radio. If you turn on your radio and the display shows frequency numbers (something like 145.010 or 431.425) instead of channels (‘Ch 7’ or just a single digit number) then that’s not an FRS/GMRS radio, and it will not work for this drill.
  • Like your NET Kit, if the cost of a radio is prohibitive, PBEM will provide one to you as a ‘permanent loan’. The deal is that it is yours as long as you maintain Active NET status, and you will return it to PBEM if/when you separate from the program. To request a ‘permanent loan’ radio (and/or NET Kit) please email net@portlandoregon.gov. If you already requested a kit, a radio will be included
